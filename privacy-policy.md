# Privacy Policy for Canada Rep Finder

**Last updated:** September 24, 2025

This privacy policy explains how Canada Rep Finder handles your information.

## Data Use & Privacy

Your privacy is our priority. The app is designed to be a privacy-respecting tool.

- **Postal Code / Location Data**: Your postal code or geographic location is used only once to determine your federal electoral district and find your representatives. This information is processed on your device and is never stored or transmitted to our servers or any third party.
- **No Analytics or Tracking**: The app does not include any analytics, advertising, or tracking software. We do not collect any information about how you use the app.
- **Recent Lookups**: To provide a convenient history, the names of representatives and their ridings from your recent lookups are stored locally on your device. You have full control over this data and can clear it at any time from within the app.

## Sources of Information

Canada Rep Finder aggregates publicly available information from the following official sources:

- [Represent by Open North](https://represent.opennorth.ca/)
- [House of Commons – Members (MPs)](https://www.ourcommons.ca/members/en/search)
- [Senate of Canada – Senators](https://sencanada.ca/en/senators/)
- [Elections Canada – Electoral District Lookup](https://www.elections.ca/content.aspx)
