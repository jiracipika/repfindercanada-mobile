Canada Rep Finder helps you discover your federal and provincial representatives in Canada. Enter a postal code or use your location to view names, titles, and contact details, then tap to call, email, copy, save recent lookups, and share. Disclaimer: This app is not affiliated with the Government of Canada, Parliament, or Elections Canada. Data is sourced exclusively from Represent by Open North. For official information, visit government websites directly. Location data is processed ephemerally and never stored.
